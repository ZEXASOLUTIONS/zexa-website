      Safety

      Safety Isn't a Section of the Proposal

      Every crew mobilises with current certifications, a reviewed job safety analysis, and a supervisor who holds stop-work authority. We report incidents, near-misses and observations on your system, in your format.

      This standard is applied on every scope, from day one.

      ESG

      Every Purchase Order Carries a Guaranteed ESG Contribution

      Responsible delivery is not a separate initiative at Zexa Solutions Group. It is built into the same methodology behind every scope: formal qualification and scoping before mobilisation, a Zero-Harm HSE standard applied to every crew and every subcontracted line, an equipment-rental model that reduces idle-asset waste instead of pushing unnecessary new purchases, and a documented, contract-administered process from RFQ through to handover. Every purchase order carries a guaranteed contribution to an ESG initiative, funded from within our margin, not billed to the client and not conditional on scope size. Being commercially registered and based in Jubail Industrial City already supports the local employment and governance transparency our clients are accountable for, and this margin-funded contribution extends that commitment into every single order we deliver.

          Privacy Notice

          This notice covers the information collected through the quote enquiry form on this site. It does not cover any other data handling by Zexa Solutions Group.

          What we collect

          Name, company, email, phone number where given, the message you enter, and any file you choose to attach.

          Why we collect it

          Solely to respond to your enquiry. We don't use it for marketing and don't sell or rent it to anyone.

          Who processes it

          Submissions are delivered to us through FormSubmit, a third-party form-relay service. FormSubmit transmits the message to our inbox and does not use it for any other purpose.

          How long we keep it

          Quote enquiries are kept for as long as needed to respond and follow up on the scope, unless you ask us to delete them sooner.
